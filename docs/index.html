Telecom 7,043 customers

Customer churn prediction

Which customers are about to leave, and what do they have in common?

Contract type beats everything else. Month-to-month customers churn at 42.7% vs. 2.8% on two-year terms, and electronic-check payers hit 45.3%. A Random Forest model got to AUC 0.84 on contract length and tenure alone.

Retention spend belongs on contract migration and payment-method switching, not on discounts spread thinly across the whole base.

Retail 9,994 transactions

Sales & profitability analysis

Revenue is growing — why isn't profit following?

Past a 40% discount, margin turns negative — some of that revenue growth was actually losing money. Tech returned 16.3% margin vs. Furniture's 8.5%, and 20% of customers drove 60% of revenue.

Cap discount authority at the point margin turns, and rebalance the range toward the categories that actually pay.

Banking 10,000 customers

Customer churn & retention strategy

Is churn a product problem or a market problem?

Both — and they split cleanly. Attrition ran 32% higher in Germany than elsewhere, while multi-product customers stuck around 40% better. The riskiest profile: single-product, high balance, gone quiet.

Cross-sell is a retention lever, not just a revenue one — and the German book needs its own diagnosis rather than a global fix.

Human resources 1,470 employees

Employee attrition modelling

Who leaves, and is it something we control?

Overtime carries 2.5× the attrition risk; higher earners are 3× less likely to leave. The pattern's clean — income −0.43, overtime +0.35, years without promotion +0.32 — and a logistic regression model called it right 85% of the time.

Attrition here is a workload and progression problem before it's a pay problem — and both are cheaper to fix than to replace people.

Fintech · risk 0.6% positive class

Credit card fraud detection

How do you model an event that almost never happens?

The hard part isn't accuracy — it's class imbalance. A model that just guesses "never fraud" scores above 99% and catches nothing. Undersampling plus RobustScaler for the outlier-heavy features made the signal usable, with V4 and V11 doing most of the work.

Tiered alerts scored by risk, so review effort lands where it pays — roughly 3× faster than flat manual review.

Insurance 1,338 policies

Medical cost & risk assessment

What actually drives a premium — who you are, or what you do?

What you do. Smoking status swamps everything else — smokers pay 3–4× more — with BMI and age as secondary factors and obesity adding another 20–30%. Lifestyle beats demographics every time.

Preventive-care programmes targeting modifiable behaviour move the cost base more than repricing by age bracket.

Automotive 5,000+ sales records

BMW global sales, 2010–2024

Where is the range actually heading over fifteen years?

EV and hybrid adoption surged 20% after 2020, with the i-Series holding its price as a halo range. Volume and margin tell different stories — the 5 Series and X3 carry 60% of units, while M-Series commands a 45% premium.

Electrification strategy and regional allocation should be planned against two separate curves, not one blended one.

How do you compare players who do completely different jobs?

With 107 metrics per player, the real work is picking which ones matter by position instead of ranking everyone on one scale. Position-specific benchmarks put Tammy Abraham at 0.5 goals per 90 and Thiago Alcántara at 89.6% pass completion — and showed defenders quietly picking up more of the attacking load.
